# Code of Conduct & Whistleblowing

This policy sets out the behaviour expected from everyone at Voltwerk Manufacturing GmbH and explains how to report concerns safely.

## Our principles

- **Respect:** We treat each other fairly, regardless of origin, language, gender, age, religion, disability or sexual identity. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated (see the General Equal Treatment Act, AGG).
- **Integrity:** We follow the law and our internal rules. We do not offer or accept bribes.
- **Safety:** We take care of our own safety and that of others.
- **Confidentiality:** We protect company and personal data.

## Gifts and invitations

- Gifts from business partners with a value of more than **EUR 50** must not be accepted, and must be reported to Compliance.
- Cash gifts are never allowed.
- Invitations to events need your manager's approval.

## Conflicts of interest

Tell your line manager and Compliance if a private interest could influence your work, for example a side job with a supplier or a close relative working at a competitor. Secondary employment needs prior written notice to HR.

## How to raise a concern

You can raise concerns in the following ways, and you can choose which suits you:

1. Talk to your line manager or shift lead.
2. Contact HR or the **works council** (Betriebsrat): betriebsrat@voltwerk.example.
3. Use the **anonymous whistleblowing channel** operated by an external provider: voltwerk-speakup.example or the free phone line 0800 000 7777. You do not need to give your name, and the system allows anonymous two-way communication.

### What happens next

- You receive an **acknowledgement within 7 days**.
- You receive **feedback within 3 months** on follow-up measures.
- Reports are handled confidentially by a small, trained Compliance team.

## Protection against retaliation

Nobody may be disadvantaged for reporting a concern in good faith. Retaliation, such as threats, dismissal or worse shift assignments, is itself a serious violation and subject to disciplinary action, in line with the Whistleblower Protection Act (HinSchG).

## Consequences

Violations may lead to warnings, termination and, where appropriate, legal action.

## Questions

Compliance Team: compliance@voltwerk.example. For personal questions you can also contact the HR Service Desk, hr@voltwerk.example.
