# Parental Leave & Elterngeld Basics

This policy gives an overview of maternity protection, parental leave (Elternzeit) and the support Voltwerk offers around the birth of a child. It applies to all employees. It is a summary: statutory law (BEEG, MuSchG) takes priority.

## Maternity protection (Mutterschutz)

- Employees are protected for **6 weeks before** and **8 weeks after** the birth. For premature or multiple births, the period after the birth is **12 weeks**.
- Please tell HR as soon as you are pregnant and willing to share it. HR will arrange a workplace risk assessment, so that tasks involving hazardous substances or heavy lifting can be adapted. This is especially important for production roles.
- During maternity protection, you receive maternity allowance from the health insurer and an employer top-up up to your regular net pay.

## Parental leave (Elternzeit)

- Each parent may take **up to 3 years** of parental leave per child, until the child's third birthday. Up to 24 months can be taken between the third and eighth birthday with the employer's agreement.
- Parental leave must be requested **in writing at least 7 weeks before** it starts (13 weeks for leave between the third and eighth birthday).
- Both parents can take parental leave at the same time.
- Employment continues, but pay stops. Vacation entitlement may be reduced by 1/12 for every full month of parental leave.
- Dismissal protection starts at the notification, at most 8 weeks before the start.

## Working part-time during parental leave

You may work **up to 32 hours per week** during parental leave. Request this in writing at least 7 weeks before. Voltwerk answers within 4 weeks. Shift employees can choose between a reduced shift model and a day-only role, depending on availability.

## Elterngeld

Elterngeld is a state benefit paid by the Elterngeldstelle of your federal state, not by Voltwerk. The amount depends on your income, your family situation and the model you choose (Basiselterngeld, ElterngeldPlus). **HR cannot calculate or promise amounts.** Please use the official calculator and information of the Federal Ministry for Family Affairs and your state's Elterngeld office.

## Voltwerk benefits for families

- **2 days of paid special leave** for partners at the birth of a child.
- A "keep in touch" programme: you can join team events and receive company news during leave.
- Return-to-work conversation 3 months before the planned return.
- Support with finding childcare through a partner service (information only).

## How to proceed

1. Tell your line manager and HR about the expected due date.
2. Submit the written parental leave request at least 7 weeks ahead.
3. Apply for Elterngeld at your state's office, ideally soon after the birth.
