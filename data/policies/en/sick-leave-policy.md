# Sick Leave & Notification

This policy explains what to do when you are unable to work because of illness. It applies to all employees.

## Who to notify and when

If you are sick, notify your team **before your shift or working day starts**.

### Shift workers

- Call your **shift lead** directly, **before the start of your shift**. If you cannot reach them, call the plant sick-line on +49 555 0100 200, which is staffed around the clock.
- A message through a colleague or a chat message alone is not sufficient.
- Give the expected duration, if you know it, so the shift can be re-planned.

### Office employees

- Notify your **line manager before 09:00** by phone or email, and inform your team.

You do not need to tell anyone your diagnosis.

## Medical certificate

- From the **4th calendar day** of illness, a medical certificate (Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung) is required. If you are sick for 3 days or fewer, no certificate is needed.
- In Germany, the certificate is transmitted electronically (**eAU**). You do not hand in a paper certificate. Your doctor sends it to your health insurer, and Voltwerk retrieves the data electronically. You must still visit the doctor in time and tell them that you are insured and employed, so that the certificate is created.
- Line managers may ask for a certificate from the first day if there is a justified reason. This is announced in writing.

## Continued pay

For up to **6 weeks** per illness, you continue to receive your regular salary (Entgeltfortzahlung). After that, your health insurer pays sickness benefit (Krankengeld). If you are sick again with a different illness, a new 6-week period starts.

## Return to work and prevention

- If you have been unable to work for more than 6 weeks within 12 months, HR will offer you a **return-to-work conversation** (BEM). Taking part is voluntary and the works council can be involved.
- You do not need a clearance certificate to return, unless required for safety-critical roles.

## Sick child

Parents may take up to 10 working days of unpaid leave per child per year for a sick child under 12, supported by a doctor's certificate. The health insurer pays child sickness benefit.
