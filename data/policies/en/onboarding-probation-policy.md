# Onboarding & Probation

This policy describes how new employees start at Voltwerk and how the probation period works. It applies to all new hires in production and in the offices.

## Before the first day

- You receive your contract documents and a welcome email with the start time and location at least 1 week in advance.
- Please bring your tax ID, social security number, bank details and, if applicable, your health insurance information.
- IT prepares your accounts. Production employees receive their access badge and locker on the first day.

## First day and first week

1. Welcome by HR and your line manager or shift lead.
2. **Mandatory safety training (2 hours) on day 1, before you start any work** in production areas. Office employees complete a shorter fire safety and first aid introduction.
3. Introduction to your team, tools and the works council.
4. Data protection and IT security e-learning, to be completed within the first week.

## Buddy programme

Every new employee gets a **buddy** for the first **3 months**. The buddy is an experienced colleague who answers everyday questions, shows you around and helps you get started. The buddy is not your line manager and has no role in your performance review. Buddies get 2 hours per week during the first month for this task.

## Probation period

- The probation period is **6 months**.
- During probation, either side can end the employment with a notice period of **2 weeks**. The notice must be in writing.
- Probation is extended automatically only in cases of long illness, if agreed in writing.
- Vacation can be taken during probation, in proportion to the months worked (2.5 days per month).

## Feedback conversations

| Time | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| After 4 weeks | First check-in and open questions |
| After 3 months | Mid-point review, buddy phase ends |
| After 5 months | Probation decision conversation |

The decision whether the employment continues is communicated **at least 2 weeks before** the end of the probation period, in writing.

## Training and development

- Production employees complete their role-specific qualifications as described in their training plan.
- All employees can book up to 3 training days per year through the learning portal after probation.

## Contact

HR Service Desk: hr@voltwerk.example. The works council welcomes new colleagues at the monthly onboarding meeting.
