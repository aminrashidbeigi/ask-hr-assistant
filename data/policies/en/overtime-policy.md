# Overtime & Working Time Account

This policy describes how working time, overtime and the working time account (Arbeitszeitkonto) work. Some rules differ between shift employees and office employees.

## Regular working time

| Group | Regular weekly working time |
| --- | --- |
| Shift employees | 35 hours |
| Office employees | 40 hours |

The legal maximum is 10 hours per day and an average of 8 hours per day over 6 months.

## What counts as overtime

### Shift employees

For shift employees, **overtime starts above 35 hours per week**. Hours beyond the rostered shift that were ordered by the shift lead are overtime from the first minute.

### Office employees

For office employees, **overtime starts above 40 hours per week**. Office employees work on trust-based working time: small deviations are not recorded as overtime. Overtime must be **approved in advance** by the line manager.

## Compensation

Overtime is not paid out by default. It is booked to the working time account and compensated with time off.

- **Shift employees:** overtime hours are credited **1 : 1.25** (for each overtime hour, 1 hour and 15 minutes are credited). Cash payment is possible only if the account limit has been reached.
- **Office employees:** overtime is credited **1 : 1**. There is no overtime supplement.

## Working time account limits

| Group | Maximum positive balance | Maximum negative balance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shift employees | +60 hours | -20 hours |
| Office employees | +100 hours | -40 hours |

When the balance reaches 80 % of the limit, you and your line manager receive an automatic notice in the HR portal, and a plan to reduce it must be agreed within 4 weeks. Hours above the limit are paid out at the end of the quarter.

## Rules for taking time off

- Time off from the account is requested like vacation, at least 1 week ahead.
- The line manager may refuse for urgent business reasons and must offer an alternative date within 4 weeks.

## Rest and Sunday work

Overtime must keep the legal rest period of 11 hours. Overtime on Sundays and public holidays is paid with the allowances from the Shift Work & Allowances policy for shift employees.

## Works council

The works council co-determines the ordering of general overtime. Individual cases are reported to it monthly.
