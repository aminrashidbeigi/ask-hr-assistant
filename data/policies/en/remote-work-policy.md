# Remote & Mobile Work

This policy applies to office employees whose tasks can be done away from the plant. Production roles on shift cannot work remotely because their tasks are tied to the line.

## Changes in version 2026-01

- Since 1 January 2026, office employees may work remotely **up to 2 days per week**. Before this date, the limit was 1 day per week.
- The monthly home office allowance was raised from EUR 15 to **EUR 25** flat rate.

## Eligibility and arrangement

- You must have completed your probation period.
- You agree the remote days with your line manager. Regular days are recorded in the team calendar.
- The manager can ask you to be on site for important meetings, workshops or in operational emergencies, with at least 24 hours of notice where possible.
- Remote work abroad is not permitted without prior approval from HR and Tax, because of social security and tax consequences.

## Working time

- Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00**. You should be reachable in this period.
- The working time rules still apply, including recording of your time and the 11-hour rest period.

## Equipment and costs

- Voltwerk provides a laptop, a headset and, on request, a monitor and an office chair for your home workplace.
- A flat rate of **EUR 25 per month** covers electricity, internet and other costs on remote days. You do not need to submit receipts.
- Private devices must not be used for company data.

## Data protection and security

- Always connect to company systems through the company VPN. Do not use public Wi-Fi without VPN.
- Lock your screen when you leave your desk, including at home.
- Do not print confidential documents at home. Do not let family members see company data.
- Report lost devices or suspected data leaks **immediately** to the IT security desk.

## Accidents at home

Accidents while working remotely are covered by statutory accident insurance in the same way as on site if they happen in direct connection with your work.
