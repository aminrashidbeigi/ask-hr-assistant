# Occupational Safety & PPE

This policy applies to everyone who works in production and warehouse areas, in particular shift employees. Safety comes before output.

## Mandatory personal protective equipment (PPE)

The following PPE is mandatory in all production areas:

- **Safety shoes**, class S3.
- **Safety glasses** with side protection.
- **Hearing protection** in all areas marked with a sound sign, which is wherever noise exceeds 85 dB(A).
- **High-visibility vest** in areas with forklift or vehicle traffic.
- **Cut-resistant gloves** when handling sheet metal, cells and sharp parts.

In battery and high-voltage areas, you additionally need **insulated gloves** and a **face shield**. Only employees with a valid high-voltage qualification may work on live components.

### Provision of PPE

PPE is provided **free of charge** by Voltwerk. Damaged or worn PPE must be replaced immediately at the PPE dispenser or through your shift lead. You must not work in the area without the required PPE.

## Rules on the shop floor

- Follow signs, floor markings and lock-out/tag-out procedures.
- Do not remove or bypass safety devices.
- Loose clothing, jewellery and open long hair are not allowed near moving machinery.
- Report hazards you see to your shift lead.

## Right to stop work

You have the right and duty to **stop work** if you believe a situation is immediately dangerous. You will not face any disadvantage for stopping work in good faith.

## Reporting incidents

### Accidents

Every work accident, however small, must be reported to your shift lead and the safety officer **within 24 hours** using the incident form in the shift app or on paper. In case of injury, first aid comes first. For emergencies, call the plant emergency number **2222** (or 112 from a mobile phone).

### Near misses

Near misses are reported **within 72 hours**. Reports of near misses are not used for disciplinary action; they help us to prevent accidents.

## Accident insurance and commuting

Work accidents and accidents on the direct way to or from work are covered by statutory accident insurance. The company doctor can be seen during your shift for work-related health issues without losing pay.

## First aiders and fire wardens

There is at least one trained first aider per shift and area. The list of first aiders and fire wardens hangs at every shift board.

## Training

Safety training is repeated every **12 months** for production employees and is mandatory. Missing training can lead to a temporary removal from production areas.
