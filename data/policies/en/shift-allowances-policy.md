# Shift Work & Allowances

This policy applies to employees in the production shift system (early, late and night shifts).

## Shift times

| Shift | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Early | 06:00 – 14:00 |
| Late | 14:00 – 22:00 |
| Night | 22:00 – 06:00 |

Shift rosters are published at least 4 weeks in advance in the shift app.

## Allowances

Allowances are paid as a percentage of your basic hourly wage for every hour worked in the relevant period.

### Night shift

The **night allowance is 25 %** for hours worked between 22:00 and 06:00.

### Sunday work

The **Sunday allowance is 50 %** for hours worked on a Sunday between 00:00 and 24:00.

### Public holidays

The **public holiday allowance is 100 %** for hours worked on a public holiday recognised in the federal state of the plant. For work on 24 December after 14:00 and on 31 December after 14:00, the 100 % rate also applies.

### Combining allowances

- The **night allowance can be combined** with the Sunday or public holiday allowance. Example: a night hour on a public holiday is paid with 25 % + 100 % = **125 %** allowance.
- The Sunday and public holiday allowances **do not add up**. If a public holiday falls on a Sunday, only the higher rate (100 %) is paid.
- Allowances are tax-free within the limits of German tax law. Payroll applies this automatically.

## Overtime in shifts

The weekly working time for shift employees is 35 hours. Overtime rules for shift staff are described in the Overtime & Working Time Account policy.

## Shift swaps

You may swap a shift with a colleague of the same qualification group.

1. Both employees agree and enter the swap in the shift app **at least 48 hours** before the earlier shift starts.
2. The shift lead approves the swap in the app.
3. The swap must keep the legal **rest period of 11 hours** between two shifts and must not lead to more than 10 hours of work per day.
4. Allowances follow the shift that is actually worked.

Swaps within 48 hours are only possible in emergencies and need the shift lead's explicit consent.

## Shift change and health

Employees aged 55 or older, or with a medical recommendation, may apply to move out of the night shift. The company doctor assesses the request together with HR.
