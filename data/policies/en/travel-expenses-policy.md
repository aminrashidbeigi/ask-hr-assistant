# Travel & Expenses

This policy applies to business trips of office employees. Production employees follow the same rules when they are sent on business trips, for example to supplier audits.

## Approval

- Every business trip must be **approved in the travel portal by your line manager before booking**, at least **5 working days before** the trip.
- Trips with expected total costs above **EUR 1,500** also require approval by the department head.
- Trips abroad outside the EU additionally need a travel safety check by Corporate Security.

## Booking

- Book transport and hotels through the travel portal only.
- **Rail** in 2nd class is the standard. Business class on trains is not reimbursed.
- **Flights** are approved when the train journey would take more than 4 hours one way. Economy class applies for flights under 6 hours.
- **Hotels** are reimbursed up to **EUR 140 per night** in Germany, including breakfast if included in the rate. Higher rates in major cities need prior approval.
- A private car may be used with approval. Mileage is reimbursed with **EUR 0.30 per kilometre**.

## Per diem (Verpflegungsmehraufwand)

Per diems follow German tax law.

| Duration of absence | Per diem (Germany) |
| --- | --- |
| More than 8 hours on one day | EUR 14 |
| Full day (24 hours) | EUR 28 |
| Arrival or departure day of a multi-day trip | EUR 14 |

Meals provided by the host or hotel (breakfast, lunch, dinner) reduce the per diem by 20 % (breakfast) or 40 % (lunch, dinner) of the full-day rate. Rates for foreign trips follow the official country table.

## Expense claims

1. Submit your expense claim in the travel portal within **30 days** after the end of the trip.
2. Attach receipts for all costs except per diems. Receipts can be photographed.
3. The line manager approves, and Finance reimburses within **14 days** after approval.

Claims submitted later than 30 days may be refused.

## What is not reimbursed

- Alcohol, minibar, entertainment and personal items.
- Fines, such as parking or traffic fines.
- Upgrades chosen for personal comfort.

## Corporate credit card

A corporate card is available for employees who travel more than 10 days per year. It is requested through the manager and Finance.
